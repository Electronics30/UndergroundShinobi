ABOUT US

Underground Shinobi is a collection of 3,333 FREE NFTs coming to Robinhood Chain.

Built around the spirit of the underground, Shinobi is more than just a collection — it’s a community-first project built with long-term vision, transparency, and trust at its core.

Every Shinobi will have its own rarity, and holders will have different ways to benefit from their NFT. You can stake your Shinobi and participate in the project's rewards, or choose to BURN your NFT once the $SHINOBI token launches and receive tokens based on your NFT's rarity.

We will provide the liquidity ourselves, with the goal of creating a system where even a Common Shinobi receives a minimum of $5 worth of tokens when burned, while higher-rarity Shinobi receive more according to their rarity.
